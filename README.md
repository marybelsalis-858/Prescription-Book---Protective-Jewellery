### Jewellery, protection & process
public research archive documenting sources, process, participation and outcomes

**Prescription Book** is an ongoing artistic research project by Marybel Salis exploring jewellery as more than decoration: as a protective object, a form of self-expression, and a carrier of memory and cultural inheritance.

My work grows from a long-standing interest in archaeology, symbols, folk and traditional culture, crystals, amulets and the protective roles jewellery has carried across time.

Sardinian knowledge and lived experience sit alongside contemporary experimentation, personalised design and hand-making.

## What is the Prescription Book?

The Prescription Book began as a place for researching and developing tailor-made protective jewellery.

Drawings, protective symbols, materials and personal stories become starting points for wearable objects.

### What would you like protection from?

This project invites other people to participate.

Protection might mean courage, memory, love, identity, strength, good fortune, connection to home — or something completely personal.

The answers can become starting points for new drawings, research and protective jewellery.
